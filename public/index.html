Different kinds of evidence.

We never turn a lead into a win. A news call is an outlet’s assessment. Certification is an election authority’s formal result.

Official vote totals

Counts published by election officials. They may be incomplete and can change. “Official source” does not mean “certified.”

News race call

A named outlet’s call, with evidence and a timestamp. Outlets may disagree or retract calls. No calls are inferred from vote shares.

Certified result

Shown only with a separate official certification record. Precincts reporting is not an estimate of ballots counted.

Source desk

Coverage is partial. Only the Minnesota adapter is enabled; it waits for poll close and rejects test results. No national news feed is connected. Unconnected states remain visible without fabricated counts.

Inventory: 2020 Census House apportionment, Senate Class II plus Florida/Ohio special elections, and the NGA governor election calendar. State governor races only; territories, delegates, primaries, runoffs and other House special elections are outside this inventory. This tracker does not project chamber control or add seats not on the ballot.
